# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members of this community pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of level of experience, gender identity, gender expression, sexual orientation, physical appearance, disability, age, race, ethnicity, religion, or socioeconomic status.

## Our Standards

Treat people with respect. Listen. Be curious, not judgmental. Assume good faith. Disagree respectfully. Give and accept constructive feedback. Take responsibility for your impact.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind
- Trolling, insulting, or deliberately provocative comments
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Sexual language or imagery not directed at the project's work
- Threats or violent language

## Enforcement

Project maintainers will address violations privately where possible. Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent removal from the community.

## Reporting

Report incidents to the project maintainer at **akshattandon4@gmail.com**. All reports will be treated with discretion.
